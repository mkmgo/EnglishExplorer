Auxiliary Verbs, Modal Verbs, and Their Properties

In British English, auxiliary verbs (helping verbs) and modal verbs work alongside main verbs to express tense, aspect, voice, mood, and negation. Below is a breakdown of these verbs, their core properties, and special negation rules.

Auxiliary verbs help main verbs form different tenses, questions, and negative sentences. They can change form according to person, number, and tense.
