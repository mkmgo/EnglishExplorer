Pocket City 2: Lesson & Activity Sheet (Part 2)

Target Audience: Beginner English Scholars (15-year-olds)

Spelling & Language: British English

Topic: Video Games & City Building (Pocket City 2)

---

Part 1: Reading Text – A Busy Day for Mayor Leo

Read the short story below about playing Pocket City 2. Pay attention to the bold words!

---

A Busy Day for Mayor Leo

It is a busy afternoon in Pocket City 2. Leo logs into the game and switches to Free Roam mode to walk around his city.

He jumps into one of his favourite vehicles—a bright yellow sports car—and drives down the main street. As he drives, he checks the town stats on his screen. The population is growing, but the citizens need more places to live.

Leo taps Back to City Mode to get a top-down view of the map. He opens the Zones menu and marks out a new residential zone for new houses. Suddenly, his phone rings with a notification:

New Quest: Mayor Sam wants you to organize a summer festival near the Town Hall.

"If I complete this quest," Leo thinks, "the citizens will be very happy, and I will earn a rare gift!"

He taps the Events menu to host the festival. Next, he uses the Inspect tool to check on the nature parks nearby. Everything looks great, but a small fire starts near an old factory! Leo quickly uses the demolish tool to clear the burnt building, then taps repair to fix the damaged roads.

After a long day, Leo levels up to Level 5 and earns a new reward for his hard work!

---

Part 2: Grammar Focus – Simple Pronouns & Articles

Pronouns replace nouns (e.g. he, she, it, they, them), while articles (a, an, the) help show which noun we are talking about.

---

Exercise 1: Choose the Correct Pronoun

Circle or write the correct word in brackets to complete the sentences.

  1. Leo drove his car through the city because he wanted to check the stats.
  2. Mayor Sam gave a quest to Leo. She asked him to host a festival.
  3. The citizens were happy because they loved the new residential zone.
  4. The old building was broken, so Leo decided to demolish it.
  5. "Will you help me repair the broken road?" asked Leo.

---

Exercise 2: Articles Practice (A / An / The)

Fill in the blanks with a, an, or the.

  1. Leo built a new residential zone for the citizens.
  2. He used the inspect tool to check the damaged building.
  3. Sam sent a quest to Mayor Leo.
  4. The player received a gift after completing the task.
  5. The sun was shining over the Town Hall.

---

Exercise 3: Complete the Dialogue
