1. Story — The Storm and the Puppy

Setting the Scene

An old oak tree stood at the end of a quiet neighbourhood. A neighbour noticed that a branch had fallen during last night's storm.

Lisa Goes Outside

She noticed something hiding under the fallen wood — a poor puppy. Lisa picked it up and brought it inside her house.

Calling the Puppy Over

She spoke with a gentle voice to call something over. The puppy wagged his tail timidly and began to approach.
