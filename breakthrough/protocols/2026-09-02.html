1. Pronouns — Fill in the Correct Pronoun

Exercise: Fill in the correct pronouns.

Yesterday, Marcus and [1] _____ met at the library to finish our history presentation. [2] _____ had brought his laptop, but I forgot [3] _____, so he shared his screen with [4] ____. After an hour of research, Sarah joined us and sat at [5] _____ table. She pulled out a stack of notes and handed [6] _____ to Marcus. "Are these notes [7] _____?" she asked him, pointing to the messy handwriting. Marcus checked the pages and replied, "No, [8] _____ are not mine; I think they belong to the students who sat here before [9] _____."

Answers

[1] me   [2] He   [3] mine   [4] me   [5] our   [6] them   [7] yours?   [8] They   [9] us
