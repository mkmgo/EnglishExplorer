Grammar Exercises

Here are some basic and clear grammar exercises covering different fields.

1. Articles (A, An, The)

Choose the correct article to fill in the blank.

  • She bought a new laptop yesterday.
  • He wants to be an engineer when he finishes university.
  • The sun rises in the east every morning.
  • We saw an old film at the cinema last night.

2. Prepositions of Place (In, On, At)

Choose the correct preposition to complete each sentence.

  • My keys are on the kitchen table.
  • Sarah lives in London with her family.
  • Please meet me at the bus stop at three o'clock.
  • The cat is sleeping i the armchair.

3. Verb Tenses (Present Simple vs. Past Simple)

Change the verb in brackets into the correct tense (Present Simple or Past Simple).

  • Every day, Tom walks to school.
  • Yesterday, we played football in the park.
  • My mother baked a delicious cake last weekend.
  • Water boils at 100 degrees Celsius.

4. Subject-Verb Agreement

Choose the correct verb form to match the subject.

  • Every student has finished their homework.
  • Cats like sleeping in warm places.
  • My brother and sister are visiting us this weekend.
  • Neither of the answers are correct.

5. Pronouns

Replace the underlined words with the correct pronoun (he, she, it, they, we).

  • He is going to the shop to buy some milk.
  • They love travelling during the holidays.
  • Look at it! It looks very fast.
  • We went to the museum on Saturday.

6. Irregular Past Tense Verbs

Exercise: Transform the Verbs — Change the present tense verb in brackets into its correct irregular past simple form.

  1. Sam met a talking black cat named Bob in the alleyway.
  2. Bob took Sam through the glowing green portal.
  3. They found a secret control room inside the grand building.
  4. Sam wore a funny costume to hide her human identity.
  5. A giant machine ran continuously to sort out the lucky crystals.
  6. Sam caught the falling crystal before it hit the floor.
