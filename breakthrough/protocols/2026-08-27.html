Daily Routine

You need to understand and apply the difference between the Present Simple tense and the Present Continuous tense.

Present Simple: Used for things that happen repeatedly. When you express routines, general occurrences, and habits, you should use this tense. Do not forget to add an -s for she, he, and it.

Present Continuous: Shows that an action is happening now and is still ongoing.

Comparison of Tenses

Every Day / Usually / Routine (Present Simple) At the Moment / Now / Still Ongoing (Present Continuous)
I wake up at seven o'clock.I am waking up.
I have breakfast before school.I am having breakfast.
I usually go to school by bus.Today I am going by car.
After school, I do my homework.I am doing my homework.
Normally I have dinner with my family.I am having dinner with my best friend.
I go to bed at ten o'clock.I am going to bed.
