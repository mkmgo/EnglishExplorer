Topic: WondLa (Season 1, Episode 1: "Doors") | Focus: Eva Growing Up & Escaping Sanctuary 573

Part 1: Reading Text — Growing Up Underground

Read the story below about Eva Nine's life, how she grew up, and her escape to the surface. Pay attention to the highlighted words!

Life with Muthr

Eva Nine grew up inside Sanctuary 573, a high-tech underground bunker designed to protect her. Because she had no human parents, she was raised entirely by Muthr, a robotic caretaker.

Muthr taught Eva everything she needed to know. Every single day followed a strict routine. Eva studied science, history, and survival techniques from computer screens and digital books. Even though Muthr was kind and deeply protective, Eva grew up feeling very lonely. She spent her childhood wondering if there were any other living humans anywhere in the universe.

Growing Up and Asking Questions

As Eva grew older, she noticed that her lessons started to change. She began asking difficult questions about why she was the only child in the facility.

On the eve of her sixteenth birthday, Eva felt ready to explore beyond her scheduled lessons. She noticed a restricted area behind a maintenance wall. When she opened the hidden door, she discovered a secret archive. Inside, she found a cryptic note with the word WondLa written on it, along with an image of a human child and strange creatures. This discovery proved to her that she was meant to live a bigger life outside the underground walls.

Stepping Onto the Surface

Suddenly, the peaceful shelter was shattered when a dangerous intruder broke into the sanctuary. An alien bounty hunter named Besteel tracked Eva down to capture her.

Muthr fought bravely against the attacker to keep Eva safe. To save her life, Muthr pushed Eva toward an emergency exit tunnel. Eva climbed up through the dark shaft and stepped through the heavy hatch.

For the very first time in her life, Eva stood under the open sky on the surface of a new planet called Orbona. Surrounded by unfamiliar plants and alien creatures, Eva began her brave journey into the unknown.
