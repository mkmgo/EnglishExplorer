Luck

The Story of Luck ...

After sharing her food with the black cat, Sam is amazed when the cat turns around and speaks to her!

The cat drops a shiny lucky coin on the ground and runs away into a dark alley. Sam picks up the coin and puts it in her pocket.

The next morning, Sam notices something incredible. Her terrible bad luck completely disappears!

  • Her toaster cooks her bread perfectly without burning it.
  • She opens her door without breaking the handle.
  • She drops her keys, but they bounce right into her hand!

Sam is thrilled. She decides to give this lucky coin to her young friend, Hazel, so Hazel can find a loving family.

However, when Sam tries to flush her toilet, she accidentally drops the lucky coin into the water! The coin flushes away forever, and Sam’s awful bad luck returns immediately.

Desperate to find another coin, Sam searches the streets and spots the black cat again. She follows the sneaky animal down a secret side street and watches him step through a glowing, green portal!

WordSimple Meaning
Lucky CoinA special metal coin that brings good fortune to anyone holding it.
AlleyA narrow street or path between buildings.
IncredibleExtremely surprising or hard to believe.
FlushTo wash something away down a toilet using water.
PortalA magical door or gateway to another world.
