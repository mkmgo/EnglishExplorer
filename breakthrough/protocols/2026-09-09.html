Pocket City 2: Lesson & Activity Sheet

Part 1: Reading Text – Welcome to Mayor's Office!

Read the short story below about playing Pocket City 2. Pay attention to the highlighted vocabulary words!

Playing Pocket City 2

Today, Leo and Sam are playing a city building game called Pocket City 2. They start at Level 1.

When they open the game, they see many choices. They can choose Free Roam to walk around the streets, or they can tap Back to City Mode to see the whole town from above.

In Free Roam, they can drive different vehicles like cars, buses, and bicycles. But right now, they need to build a thriving city!

Leo taps the Inspect button to look at a small house. "Look," he says, "this family needs a park."

Sam agrees and opens the Town Hall menu. As the Mayor of the city, Sam needs to check the Stats to see how happy the citizens are.

Suddenly, a notification pops up on the screen:

New Quest: Anna wants you to build 10 residential zones.

"If we complete this quest," Sam says, "she will give us a special gift!"

They open the Zones tab to construct new areas. They also open the Buildings tab to add a fire station, and the Nature tab to plant trees and flowers in the park.

Later, a big thunderstorm hits the town! Some roads are damaged. Sam has to repair the broken bridge, whilst Leo decides to demolish an old, abandoned factory to make space for a new playground.

They also organise fun community events, like a summer festival near the Town Hall, so everyone in town stays happy!
