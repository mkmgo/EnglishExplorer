1. Simple Present and Present Continuous

By using usually and now, it becomes very clear what Simple Present and Present Continuous mean.

Usually my brother goes to bed at 9 o'clock.
→ Now he is going to bed.

Exercise: Write three sentences in your exercise book using these phrases: to brush your teeth, to play, and to go to school.
