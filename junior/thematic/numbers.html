Numbers 1 – 1000

Every number is made of digits in different places:

Thousands 1 = 1000
Hundreds 3 = 300
Tens 4 = 40
Ones 5 = 5
1000 + 300 + 40 + 5 = 1345
→ "One thousand, three hundred and forty-five"
